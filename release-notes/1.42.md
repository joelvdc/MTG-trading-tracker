# 1.42: compact decks, folding sections, the maybeboard and adding cards from the deck page
- **Add card on the deck page:** the "+ Add card" button opens card search (and from there the scanner) for that deck. Cards you pick are added in this app only, under "Added in app", like scanned cards sent "To deck"; Archidekt isn't changed and they stay when the deck is reloaded. Searching needs internet.
- **Compact view for decks:** the button next to ⟳ (or ⋮ → Compact view) shows one slim line per card, without pictures, like the collection's compact view. The choice is kept for every deck.
- **Fold sections away:** tap a heading (a category, or a card type when grouped by type) to hide its cards; tap it again to show them. ⋮ → Collapse all / Expand all sections does them all at once. Folded headings stay folded, in every deck that has them.
- **Show maybeboard (⋮):** the deck's maybeboard on Archidekt, and any other category Archidekt leaves out of the deck (a sideboard, for example), appears under the deck in its own section, with prices. It's never counted in the deck's cards, value, "Cards I'm missing", "Add to collection" or what the trade binder must keep. Decks get their maybeboard the next time they're loaded from Archidekt (⟳); it's saved on the phone, so it shows offline too.
- **Update both phones:** the maybeboard is synced with the deck, so 1.42 uses a new sync file format. Install 1.42 on both phones before they sync again; a phone still on 1.41 or older will ask to be updated. Backups made by 1.42 also need 1.42 to restore.

`MTG-Trader-1.42.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.42-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
