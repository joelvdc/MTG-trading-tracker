# 1.45: choose which printing of a token a deck shows
- **Other printings:** tap a token in a deck's Tokens section and then **Other printings** to see every printing Scryfall has of it, newest first, each with its set and number. The printing your deck's cards link to comes first, marked "the cards' own".
- **Your pick stays with the deck:** tap a printing and the deck shows it from then on, in the Tokens section and when you open the token ("Your pick for this deck"). Each deck keeps its own pick per token, also after reloading the deck from Archidekt. **Use the cards' own printing** goes back.
- **Synced and offline:** picks sync to your other phone with your other settings (update both phones to 1.45). Choosing needs internet; the picked pictures are saved for offline use with your decks' other pictures. Tap the big picture to see it full screen.

`MTG-Trader-1.45.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.45-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
