# 1.38: received cards, wishlist sections, swiping and selecting cards
- **Wishlist sections:** the wishlist shows To buy, Ordered and Received; tap a section's header to fold it away. Prefer one list? Switch off "Ordered cards in their own section" in the view menu (top right): ordered cards then go to the bottom.
- **Swipe right = Received:** once a card has arrived, swipe it to the right (or tick "Received" in its window). It moves to Received with the date, as a record: no longer counted, starred in trades, alerted or shared. Swipe a received card right again to put it back on the wishlist.
- **Swipe left = Delete,** with Undo. A swipe only counts once it's gone 40% of the way, and the colour behind the card shows what it will do.
- **New in the wishlist's ⋮ menu:** "Mark all ordered cards as received", "Mark the cards I now own as received" (it replaces "Remove the cards I got since adding them", shows the cards first, and is explained when greyed out), and "Clear received cards".
- **Received cards are kept** until you clear them. Settings → Received wishlist cards can clear them after 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, and can add received cards to the collection (off by default, since order imports add them).
- **Selecting cards:** press and hold a card (or ⋮ → Select cards), then tap more. On the wishlist: Ordered, Received, Delete, Back on the wishlist, Share. In the collection: Move to binder, Add to a trade, Delete (with a restore point first), Edit condition, language and price, Share or copy as a list, Export as CSV. "Select all shown" follows your search and filters.
- **Swiping in the collection:** right = Move to binder…, left = Add to a trade… Settings → Swiping cards in the collection lets you pick another action for each direction: trade binder in/out, one copy more or less, add to the wishlist, or nothing.
- **Undo everywhere:** every swipe and every action on selected cards can be undone right after.
- **Update both phones:** received cards sync, so the sync file (and backups) went to a new version. A phone still on 1.37 or older stops syncing and asks to be updated.

`MTG-Trader-1.38.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.38-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
