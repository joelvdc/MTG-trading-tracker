# 1.28: prices in kroner or dollars
- **Settings → Currency:** show every price in euros, Danish kroner or US dollars. Cardmarket's euro prices are converted with the European Central Bank's daily rates, which the app keeps for when you're offline (Settings shows the rate and its date).
- Everything follows the choice: the collection, card pages, search, trades, the value chart, stats, the wishlist and the trade binder. The value filter, the trade binder's minimum value, and the purchase and agreed prices you type use it too.
- The app still keeps prices in euros: CSV files, CardTrader imports and Archidekt are unchanged. The currency choice syncs to your other phones.

`MTG-Trader-1.28.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.28-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
