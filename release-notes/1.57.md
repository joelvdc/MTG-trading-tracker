# 1.57: hold − or + for 10 commander damage
- **Fixed: holding − or + in the commander damage window now changes the damage by 10**, and again by 10 about every 0.7 seconds while you keep holding, as on a life panel. Before, a hold there did nothing.
- It follows Settings → Life tracker → hold ("By 10" or "By 1, fast"), like the life panels. A tap still changes it by 1; it never goes below 0, and life follows when commander damage takes life.
- With partner commanders each half works on its own. A short hint under the boxes says so.
