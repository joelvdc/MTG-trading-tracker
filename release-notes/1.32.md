# 1.32: order files from Cardmarket, package check, purchase prices
- **Sort by value change:** the collection's Sort has two new orders, "Change in %" and "Change in money", biggest rise or biggest fall first, with shortcuts "Rising lately" and "Falling lately". The change is Cardmarket's trend price against its 30-day average for one copy; cards without a 30-day average go last.
- **Rising and falling per card:** the value screen's "Rising lately" and "Falling lately" now rank one copy's change too (before, the change over all copies you own), so both screens agree.
- **Cardmarket orders:** "Import an order" reads Cardmarket's files as well as CardTrader's: a shipment's "ArticlesFromShipment" CSV (condition, language, foil, signed, altered; a playset counts as four cards) and the monthly "Purchased Articles" CSV.
- **Check a package against its order:** on the scanned cards list, "Check an order" compares what you scanned with the order's file: missing cards, wrong language, wrong printing and cards that weren't ordered. Mark a card "It's here" when the scanner got it wrong, copy a problem report for the shop, and add the cards to a binder with the order's price, condition and finish.
- **Purchase prices from orders:** ⋮ → "Purchase prices from orders…" sets what you paid on cards already in the collection, from one or several order files, with a preview first. A card gets the average paid, weighted by copies; existing prices are kept unless you choose to overwrite.
- **Average when buying more:** importing an order that adds copies to a stack that already has a purchase price now stores the average, weighted by copies (before, the old price was kept).

`MTG-Trader-1.32.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.32-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
