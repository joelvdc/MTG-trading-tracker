# 1.53: combos and tutors in the win turn
- **Combos now count by what they really do.** 1.52 only counted combos Commander Salt marks as winning *and* needing nothing else on the battlefield, which left out all of Auntie Plague's. Now:
  - combos that **win outright** count, like Soul Immolation + Tree of Perdition (Tree has to be out since the turn before, as its {T} ability needs);
  - **endless loops** of deaths, creatures entering or -1/-1 counters count when the deck has a card that turns them into life loss: Grave Venerations or The Meathook Massacre for deaths, Obelisk Spider for -1/-1 counters you put (Devoted Druid + Mikaeus, Blowfly Infestation's loops), Purphoros and the like for creatures entering;
  - **endless tokens** (High Perfect Morcant + Flourishing Defenses) attack and win the turn after;
  - what a combo needs first is checked too: other creatures on the battlefield, a card that can start the loop, an opponent's creature (taken as there from turn 4).
- **Tutors** (Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Worldly Tutor…, not land searches) fetch the missing piece of the combo closest to done.
- Combo pieces that are instants or sorceries wait in hand until the rest of the combo is there.
- **Auntie Plague:** now wins by combo in about 1 in 4 games (by turn 8 in 9% of games, by turn 10 in 18%); it still usually wins by turn 11. Your other decks barely change.
- **How?** under Winning the game lists the combos that can win and what they need ("with Obelisk Spider…").
- The app fetches each deck's combo details from Commander Salt again once (for what the combos need first); the win turns are worked out again.

`MTG-Trader-1.53.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.53-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
