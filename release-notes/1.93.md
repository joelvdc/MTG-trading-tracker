# 1.93: TCGplayer and Card Kingdom prices from the website
- **TCGplayer prices in a second or two** when you're logged in to your MTG Trader account: the app gets them from the website in one go, instead of asking Scryfall 75 cards at a time. Cards the website doesn't know yet, or whose price is more than two days old, still come from Scryfall.
- **Card Kingdom prices from the website too**, like Cardmarket's since 1.81: the same list the website shows, a smaller download, and nothing downloaded when the list hasn't changed. Settings → Prices says "from the MTG Trader website". Saving them on the phone takes as long as before.
- If the website can't be reached, or you have no account, everything works as before (Scryfall, Card Kingdom, GitHub's copy).
- Android's own Google backup of the app is switched off. The app's own backups (daily on the phone or in your chosen folder, the website's last 10 days, the nightly copy to Nextcloud) are unchanged. Moving to a new phone by copying from the old one still brings the data along; log in to your account again there.
