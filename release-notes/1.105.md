# 1.105: ScrollVault removed
- **ScrollVault is gone** from the app and the website: it can no longer be picked as the power level source, and its
  power level, win turns and "At the table" section no longer show on deck pages or rule-zero cards. If ScrollVault was
  your choice, Commander Salt's power level shows instead (Settings → Commander deck power level to pick another).
  ScrollVault's terms forbid automated requests, and the app used its page in a hidden browser.
- **Clearer errors**: when EDHREC's or Archidekt's answer can't be read, the app now says "EDHREC's answer has changed"
  or "Archidekt's answer has changed" with the reason, instead of a technical error.
- **Website**: the same ScrollVault removal. It now notes whether its own requests to EDHREC and Archidekt (made when
  you open Recommendations or import or reload a deck) worked, so the weekly health check no longer sends requests to
  those two services itself.
- No sync format or database change: phones on 1.104 keep syncing, and ScrollVault ratings they already have are kept
  in the sync file, just not shown.
