# 1.89: share links, legality check, printing and a weekly email
- **Share links:** "Share a link to this deck" in a deck's menu, a Share button on a recorded game, and "Share collection" / "Share this binder" in the Collection menu. The app syncs, asks the website for the link and opens Android's share menu. Anyone with the link sees a read-only page on the website with your prices (and where they come from), never what you paid; a game's note stays private. Sharing needs an MTG Trader account.
- **Commander legality check** on the deck page: "Commander legal ✓", or what's wrong (banned cards, cards outside the commander's colours, extra copies, not 100 cards, a commander that can't lead or two that can't pair up). The maybeboard doesn't count. Decks with a problem get a ⚠ on the Decks tab. The rules come from Scryfall and are kept on the phone for a week.
- **Website:**
  - **Share** buttons on the deck page, the game window and the Collection page; **Settings → Shared links** lists every link, switches it off or on again (the same address comes back) and deletes it. Links never expire on their own.
  - **Print** on the Collection page (what the page shows: binder, filters, search and sorting, all of it) and on the deck page: a clean A4 list without pictures, to save as PDF. "Include purchase prices" for the collection, off by default.
  - The same **legality check** on the deck page, and "⚠ Not legal" on the Decks page.
  - **Settings → Weekly email:** pick an address (confirmed by a link) and a day of the week. The email shows the collection's value against a week ago, the 5 cards you own that went up most and the 5 that went down most this week, wishlist cards that reached your alert price, and changes in your decks (game changers added or removed, new legality problems). Gainers and losers start once the website has saved a week of daily prices. Every email has a link to stop them.
