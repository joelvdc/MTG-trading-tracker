# 1.95: a calmer deck page, with its last modified date
- **A deck's page shows when it was last modified**, under its cards, value and owner: for Archidekt decks, the date Archidekt last changed it; for local decks, the last change made on a phone or on the website. It's the same date the website shows. The Decks tab is unchanged.
- **Much less help text on the deck page**: the "Edit this deck on Archidekt…" note is gone, the "Showing the printings you own…" line is gone (switching printings stays in the ⋮ menu), and so are the bracket explanation in the scores card, the colours explanation in the stats, the "Tap a token…" note, "Only in this app so far" and the maybeboard's explanation.
- **Scores card**: the power line is shorter, and "Scored <date>" and a short Refresh button share one line.
- **Legality**: one line, "Commander legal ✓", or "Not Commander legal" in red with the problems under it.
- **Maybeboard**: no separate big title any more: a divider, then its folding sections.
- **Stats**: shorter footers ("% of games · 10,000 shuffles"); the "best case" explanation moved inside How?; "n cards still loading from Scryfall"; and Games shows the opponents' average bracket and power as one row.
- **Retired decks** say just "Retired" next to Bring back.
