# 1.112: Forgot password, two-step login
- **Forgot password?** on the website's login page and in the app's login: type your user name or email and a link arrives by email (it works once, for an hour). Setting a new password logs out every device.
- **Email at sign-up**: new accounts give an email address, confirmed by a link. It is the same address as the weekly email (Settings → Email), which stays off until you turn it on. Existing accounts: add yours under Settings → Email so a reset can reach you.
- **Two-step login** (optional): Settings → Two-step login on the website. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, then logging in on a new browser or phone also asks for its 6-digit code. You get 10 recovery codes, each working once. A password reset by email keeps it on; the owner's reset turns it off (the way back for a lost phone).
- Older app versions can't log in to an account with two-step login on, and can't make new accounts: update the app.
