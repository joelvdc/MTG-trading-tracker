# 1.48: the life tracker as Lifetap
- **New look:** soft colours with dark text (or commander pictures), each player's name on a label, the life total with room around it, − and + at the ends, and a commander damage mini-grid on every panel: a tiny copy of the table with "me" on your seat and each opponent's damage in their colour (partners split in two).
- **A menu bar across the middle** in place of the round button: end the game, Game settings (the circle with the number of players), the clock (tap to pause), day/night, history (hold to undo), the mana pool and the dice (hold for a d20 at once).
- **Each player's sheet** (tap their name), facing them: Counters, with commander damage on a seating grid (tap +1; hold a box for − and +, and a Partner commanders switch for two counts) and tiles for poison, commander tax, monarch, initiative, day/night, the city's blessing, energy, experience, rad, treasure, your own counters and K.O.; Player, to change the player or the deck during the game, the background (picture or colour), contrast, or concede.
- **Game settings in the game:** players, layouts with a player at each end (4 and 6 players), starting life, seats and decks, commander damage and its life loss, what happens when a player is down (ask "Dead?", out at once, or nothing), turns and clock, the game log, the controls, vibration, colours or pictures, coloured mini-grids, three panel layouts, the low-life animation, an auto-hiding bar, dice animations, screen on and full screen. They're kept for the next games; Settings → Life tracker now points here.
- **Dice & random, mana pool, history:** a random player or opponent, a coin, a custom die and d4 to d20 (shown twice so both sides of the table can read them); WUBRG, colourless and storm; the game's log with undo and pause, and the past games.
- **Ending a game** is one window over the table: who won (or a draw) and how, then Save and rematch at once, Save then new players, or end without saving.
- **New game** is shorter: players, seats and decks, and who starts. Plain colours are now the default background.

`MTG-Trader-1.48.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.48-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
