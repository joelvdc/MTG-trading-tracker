# 1.114: Brackets and salt for your own decks, picture views on the website
- Decks made in the app (and on the website) now get an **estimated bracket and salt**, worked out by the website from
  Commander Spellbook's combos, Scryfall's card tags and MTGJSON's salt scores. Commander Salt still scores Archidekt decks.
- The **bracket card** says **why** a deck is in its bracket (for example "Thassa's Oracle + Demonic Consultation:
  wins, mana value 3"), and its combos show whether they win and their mana value. Rules used: a two-card combo that
  wins for 8 mana or less, 4 or more Game Changers, mass land denial or 3+ extra-turn cards make bracket 4; any Game
  Changer, any other two-card combo or 5+ tutors make bracket 3.
- The **power card** of an app-rated deck shows its salt total, level (mild, moderate, high) and saltiest cards.
- Decks tab: Archidekt decks of other people are now under **Archidekt (other users)**.
- Website: deck pages have **Stacks** and **Grid** views next to the list; Settings → Website backups has
  **Download a copy now**, and the website keeps 3 days of backups (Nextcloud copies stay at 10).
