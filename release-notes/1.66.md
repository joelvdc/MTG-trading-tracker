# 1.66: the app's own power level; the collection opens during price downloads
- **New power level source "This app"** (Settings → Commander deck power level): a whole number from 1 to 10, worked out on the phone from the deck's win turn and Commander Salt's ratings. It's used everywhere the power level shows: the Decks tab and its sorting, the deck page, the power level cards and the printed PDF.
- **How it's worked out:** the turn by which half the simulated games are won (with decimals, e.g. turn 8.6), put on the turn scale of the r/EDH "Guide to Power Levels in EDH" (turn 6 is 8, turn 8 is 7, turn 10.5 is 6, turn 13 is 5), plus Commander Salt's interaction, consistency, efficiency and mana base ratings, at most one point either way, then rounded. The deck page shows how it came about.
- The first time, the win turns are worked out in the background (a few seconds per deck, shown above the decks, and it can be stopped). They're kept on the phone after that and worked out again only when a deck changes.
- **Loading circle during price downloads:** the collection no longer waits with a loading circle while prices download. Card Kingdom's prices and Cardmarket's price guide and product list are now saved in small batches instead of all at once, so the collection can load in between.
