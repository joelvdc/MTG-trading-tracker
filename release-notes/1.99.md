# 1.99: clickable cards in primers
- **Card links in a primer**: write a card's name in double brackets, like `[[Sol Ring]]`, and it becomes a link. On the app's Primer page, tap it to open that card's window (the same one as in the deck list); closing it brings you back to the primer.
- **On the website**: type `/` in the primer editor and a list of the deck's cards pops up; it narrows as you type (`/sol`), and Enter or a click puts the link in. Linked cards are clickable in the primer and in Preview, and show the card's picture on hover when "Card on hover" is on. A link to a card that isn't in the deck shows as a bold name.
- **Write with AI** now links the deck's cards by itself, and after writing it tells you how long it took, how many tokens it wrote (and how many it spent thinking) and the tokens per second.
- The AI primer also copes better with thinking models: it gets more room to answer, and says so when it ran out.
- No sync change: phones on 1.97 and 1.98 keep syncing.
