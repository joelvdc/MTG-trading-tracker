# 1.61: Marionette Master and friends in the win turn
- **Collection opens during price downloads:** Card Kingdom's daily price list is now downloaded first and saved after, so the Collection page no longer shows only a loading circle while it downloads.
- **Sacrifice payoffs:** with Mirkwood Bats, Marionette Master or Marionette Apprentice on the battlefield, the win turn now sacrifices the Treasures left over after casting, cracks Food and Maps with the mana left, and uses Sai's "sacrifice two artifacts: draw a card". Nuka-Cola Vending Machine's Treasures and Worldwalker Helm's Maps are counted. Tawnos's sacrifice ability sets them off too.
- **Impact Tremors** and other "whenever a creature you control enters" damage now count.
- **Populate** (Nesting Dovehawk) copies your best creature token, and Dovehawk grows when creature tokens enter.
- **Several tokens are several tokens:** Whirler Rogue's two Thopters each count for Mirkwood Bats, Kambal and lords like Urza (before, they were one token with all the power). Token copies also count as tokens made.
- **Tawnos's copies:** a copy from his second ability is an artifact (so Urza, Wanderglyph and Cybermen Squadron apply), and copying a token of a card keeps the card's abilities.
- **Illustrious Wanderglyph's** +2/+2 only once you control ten permanents (the city's blessing); its Gnome tokens count as a token maker in "How?".
- **Urza, Prince of Kroog:** 0-power artifact creatures like Ornithopter are now cast when a lord can pump them, and Urza's {6} ability copies your best artifact as a 1/1.
- Win turns are worked out again once, so decks with these cards may show a different turn.
