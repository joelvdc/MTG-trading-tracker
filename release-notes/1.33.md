# 1.33: order history, paid and worth, precons, condition-adjusted values
- **Orders:** ⋮ → "Orders" in the collection remembers what you bought: every order you import, package you check and precon you add, with its date, what you paid and what its cards are worth now. "+" adds the files of earlier orders (several at once); they only go into this list.
- **Paid and worth:** the collection value screen compares what you paid with what the cards are worth now, for all cards or one binder, and lists your best and worst buys. Only cards with a purchase price count.
- **Add a preconstructed deck:** ⋮ → "Add a preconstructed deck…": pick a Commander deck, the binder, what you paid and the day. The cards are added without basic lands, each with its share of the price (split by what the cards are worth today), and the deck becomes an order.
- **Count a card's condition in its value:** a new switch in Settings, off until you turn it on. A copy then counts for a percentage of the card's price (Excellent 85%, Good 70%, Light Played 60%, Played 45%, Poor 30%; adjustable) everywhere a value is shown, and the price is marked, e.g. "EX 85%".
- **Update both phones:** orders are kept in the sync file, so a phone still on an older version stops syncing and asks to be updated. Update every phone before the next sync.

`MTG-Trader-1.33.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.33-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
