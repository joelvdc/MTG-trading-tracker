# 1.119: Combos that need a third card

- Some Commander Spellbook combos need "any card of a kind" besides their named cards, for example Springheart Nantuko
  + Tireless Provisioner + any man-land that enters untapped. These counted as two-card combos for the bracket; they
  now count as three-piece combos (blue, tagged C3+) and the extra piece is named in the combo line.
- Every deck's bracket is worked out again once, so decks that only had such a combo may drop a bracket.
- Website: the same fix on the deck pages and the bracket card.
