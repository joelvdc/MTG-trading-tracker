# 1.107: Sort with your decks: a Decks binder, your own levels, copies kept together
- **Only your active decks hold cards**: your own Archidekt decks (the ones under your Archidekt username) and your local decks, as long as they aren't retired. Decks of other Archidekt users are followed decks: you still see which of their cards you own on their deck page, but they no longer keep cards out of the sort, the trade binder or the website's Spare filter. The app no longer guesses your username from the last deck you imported: set it in Settings → Decks → Archidekt (the Decks tab and the Sort screen remind you when it's empty).
- **A Decks binder**: the sort can fill one binder with the copies your active decks use. A new deck's cards show up under "For your decks"; a retired or deleted deck's cards go back to your binders (or to Unsorted when there's no room). Add deck to collection now offers the Decks binder, and a precon's "All cards".
- **Best copies in your decks**: choose "Best copy first" (the default) or "Cheapest first". When a better copy turns up, the sort suggests swapping it into the Decks binder and putting the old one back. Moves into and out of the Decks binder don't count against the weekly limit.
- **Your own levels**: 1 to 6 binders, best first, each with your own name, how many copies it holds and an optional minimum price (new setups start with Best cards, Good cards and Bulk). Your existing three binders carry over as they are.
- **Keep copies together**: all printings of a card (the default), only the same printing, or off. A copy worth at least 3 times more and at least €5 more than the usual one goes its own way (both numbers can be changed). A copy coming back joins its group, and a weaker card makes room.
- **Sort everything now**: ignore this week's limit for one sort, e.g. after taking a deck apart. Cards that fit nowhere stay in Unsorted marked "no room", with a note on what to do.
- **Filter and sort the collection by deck**: "Used by deck" in the filter and a "Deck" sort in the app; a Deck column and the same filter on the website.
- **On the website (live with this release)**: the Sort page has the same settings and sections. A phone on 1.106 or older keeps syncing and keeps sorting with its old three-binder setup.
