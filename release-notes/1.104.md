# 1.104: Sort the collection, and find the cards you need
- **Sort the collection** (Collection ⋮ → Sort the collection…): pick or create three binders (Collection binder, Not bulk and Bulk), say how many copies each may hold (Bulk can be unlimited) and the price under which a card is always bulk. The app ranks your cards by value and Commander popularity (a slider sets how much popularity counts) and suggests where each copy goes. Cards your decks use, the trade binder and all your other binders are left alone. Tick what you moved and tap Done; moves you leave unticked aren't suggested again.
- **Gentle weekly updates**: the first sort places everything at once. After that, a card only moves when it clearly belongs elsewhere (it must beat the weakest card in the better binder by 25%), a card that moved stays put for 8 weeks, and at most 10 copies a week move between your binders (you set the number). New cards coming in from Unsorted don't count against that limit. ⋮ → Start over sorts everything again from scratch.
- **Find cards** (Collection ⋮ → Find cards…, or a deck's ⋮ → Find these cards…): paste a list or pick a deck and see where every copy is stored, binder by binder, so you empty one binder at a time. Tick cards off as you pull them; the list stays until you clear it. Copies that only other decks use and cards you don't own are listed apart, with "Add all to wishlist". Basic lands are skipped unless you switch that off.
- **On the website (live with this release)**: the same two pages, under Collection → More ▾ (Sort the collection, Find cards). The deck page has "Find these cards" in Edit ▾, and Select mode has a "Find" button for the ticked cards. The weekly email gets a "Sort the collection: N moves this week" section once the first sort is done.
- No sync format change: the sort's settings travel with the other synced settings, so a phone still on 1.103 keeps syncing (it just doesn't show the new screens).
