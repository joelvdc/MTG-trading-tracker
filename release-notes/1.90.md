# 1.90: MDFC filter, a Retry button for prices, and choosable columns on the website
- **Faces filter:** the collection's Filter has a new part, "Faces": MDFC (modal double-faced cards such as the Pathways and the Zendikar Rising spell/lands), Transforming (Delver, werewolves) and Split / Adventure. Combine it with "Only cards in my decks" to see which MDFCs your decks use. After the update the app looks up your collection's card details once more in the background (about a minute).
- **Retry** on the "Price update failed" and "Price download failed" notes above the tabs: it starts the same update as Settings → "Update prices now", no need to open Settings.
- **Website:**
  - The same **Faces** filter on the Collection page.
  - The wishlist's picture view has the **Ordered** tick box under each card, like the table and the app.
  - **Columns** (in the table view): choose which columns the collection table shows and in what order; remembered in that browser, "Reset to default" brings back the usual ones. New columns: number, rarity, type, mana value, colours, colour identity, faces, EDHREC rank, Game Changer, finish, signed/altered, notes, change in money, paid, profit, each price source and what Card Kingdom pays, and three about decks: **Decks** (how many of your decks use the card; point at the number for their names), **In decks** (copies they use) and **Spare** (copies you own over every printing, minus copies in decks; red when your decks need more than you have). Decks, Spare and EDHREC rank can also be sorted on.
