# 1.98: a Primer page in the app, AI-written primers on the website
- **Primer page**: the deck page now shows the primer as one short line ("Primer: …" or "Add a primer"). Tap it, or ⋮ → Deck → Primer…, to open the primer on its own page in larger type, easy to read at the table. The pencil opens the editor, and saving offers Undo.
- **On the website**: the primer has moved to the bottom of the deck page ("Primer ↓" next to the role line takes you there). You can write it right there with a preview, no pop-up window, and the browser asks before you leave with unsaved changes.
- **Write with AI** (website, in the primer editor): pick a focus (Casual, Upgraded, High power, cEDH), a length and the sections you want, then let the AI write a primer or improve yours. It reads the decklist, roles, bracket, power, combos and your card notes, never your prices or collection. The text lands in the editor as a draft; nothing is saved until you press Save. Up to 20 a day.
- No sync change: phones on 1.97 keep syncing.
