# 1.67: tidier bracket and power level cards
- **"4 in 10 games" is gone** from the bracket cards (the "Wins by" tile on the printed card, the Speed section in the app) and from the deck page's **Winning the game** headline: they now say "Wins by turn 10". The README explains what the turn means.
- **Printed bracket cards** show only the bracket number in the Baseline and Realistic tiles (no more "Core", "Upgraded" and so on); the cards in the app keep the names.
- **Bracket cards' footer** says "Bracket data: Commander Salt".
- **Power level cards' footer** is on two lines, so it no longer gets cut off: "Power level: MTG Trader app" (or the site the power level comes from) and "Data: Commander Salt".
- **Saltiness** on the printed power level card wraps onto a second line instead of being cut off (e.g. "mostly community salt ratings").
