# 1.58: life tracker feedback
- **Vibration you can feel:** the life tracker now runs the phone's vibration motor itself, as Lifetap does, so it works in silent mode and with the phone's touch vibration switched off. A short buzz for life + and −, commander damage, poison and other counters, and the mana pool; a longer one for dice, coins, random picks and undo. The Haptic feedback switch still turns it off.
- **Who starts:** after the random pick, the player who starts has a gold "1st" badge on their name label until the first change in the game (life, commander damage, poison, a counter). With turns counted, the gold turn badge already shows it.
- **"Drain / burn"** is a new answer to "How was it won?", at the end of a game and on a past game's page.
- **Change who played which deck in a past game:** on a game's page, tap a player to pick another player (or a guest) or another deck. The result stays; a different deck brings its bracket and power level of today.
- **Guests can pick a commander** (and a partner) instead of a deck. Their panel gets the commander's picture and colours, partners get two commander damage counts, and in the stats the commander counts like a deck.
- **"Anonymous":** in Game stats → Decks, the seats where nobody picked a deck or commander are now one row called Anonymous (it had no name).
- **Life tracker settings in Settings:** Settings → Life tracker → Life tracker settings has the settings for every game (controls, vibration, look, the phone) without starting a game.
- On a deck's page, the **Games** section now comes before **Tokens**.
