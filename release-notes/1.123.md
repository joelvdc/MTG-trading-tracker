# 1.123: Win turns shared with the website, combo brackets

- Logged in to an MTG Trader account, the app takes the win turns the website already worked out, so most decks show
  theirs straight away instead of being played on the phone. One is only taken when the deck's list, colours, rating
  and simulator version are the same as on the phone; the rest are still worked out here, and those go up to the
  website so it doesn't play them again. The website now also starts on a deck's win turn as soon as the phone syncs
  a change.
- Every combo line (the deck page's Combos section and a card's own combos), in the app and on the website, shows
  Commander Spellbook's tag with its bracket range: "Spicy (bracket 3-4)", "Ruthless (bracket 4+)", "Exhibition (any
  bracket)".
- Website: its price and card files are downloaded again only when they changed, which cuts most of its use of
  Vercel's free Blob transfer.
