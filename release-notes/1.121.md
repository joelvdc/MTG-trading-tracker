# 1.121: Win turn: blinking combo pieces and every starting zone

- Win turn: a combo piece that has to enter, already on the battlefield, starts its combo again when a blink card makes
  it leave and come back (its enter trigger fires again; it is not cast). Ondu Spiritdancer cast before Secret Arcade
  now still wins with Teleportation Circle or Thassa, Deep-Dwelling at the end step, or Ephemerate in hand.
- Every place Commander Spellbook lets a piece start now counts: hand, command zone, battlefield, graveyard (with its
  "at least six other cards in your graveyard"), library (Kiki-Jiki fetched by Goblin Recruiter) and exile (Dramatic
  Reversal exiled by Isochron Scepter as both are cast).
- A piece that may start in hand or the command zone (High Perfect Morcant) also has to enter during its combo now.
- More blink cards are read: "exile target creature you control, then return it" (Ephemerate) and "up to one other
  target" (Thassa, Deep-Dwelling).
- Every deck's rating and win turn are worked out again once. On the Ready to Play decks: Zur's combo share goes from
  9% to 11% (its two blink cards), Auntie Plague's from 39% to 38% (Morcant out first no longer counts).
- Website: the same win turn.
