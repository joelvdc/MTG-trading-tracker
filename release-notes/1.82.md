# 1.82: game stats overview, lending decks, Nextcloud only for backups, Settings in groups
- **Game stats Overview:** Games → Stats opens on a new **Overview** tab: games played, your win rate, the average game and number of turns, games per month, how games were won, and how often the player who started wins (against an equal chance). A period choice above the tabs (all time, last 12 months, last 3 months) applies to every tab.
- **Lend your decks:** friends and guests can play one of your decks. In the seat picker, "Your decks (lent)" lists your decks; a deck already at another seat is greyed out. The game counts for the deck (its results show "N lent" and "Lent: N games, W won", the last games show "played by Kim"), while the win or loss goes to the player who borrowed it. "You won" and head to head only count the games you played yourself. The website's game window has the same choice.
- **Nextcloud only for backups:** the app now syncs only through your MTG Trader account. A phone that still synced with Nextcloud shows "Nextcloud sync has ended: log in with your MTG Trader account" with **Switch to my account**; nothing on Nextcloud is deleted. App backups stay on the phone (or in a folder you choose); the website copies your account to Nextcloud every night, and the Backups screen links to the website's Settings. Backups the app kept on Nextcloud before can still be restored with "Restore from a file…".
- **Settings in groups:** the main Settings page is now a list (Account & sync, Prices, Collection & trades, Wishlist, Decks, Games, Backups, Appearance & offline, About), each with a line saying what it's set to, opening its own page. All settings are the same as before.
