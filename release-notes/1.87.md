# 1.87: a new logo
- **New app icon.** "Card Swap": two cards trading places inside a circle of arrows, in MTG Trader's purple and gold. It stays whole whatever shape your phone gives icons (circle, rounded square, teardrop).
- **Themed icons.** With Android 13+ "Themed icons" turned on, the icon now has its own one-colour version, so the two cards stay apart instead of merging into one shape.
- **Website:** the same logo is now the browser-tab icon, the icon when you add the website to an iPhone or iPad home screen, and sits beside the name in the top bar and on the login page.
- Some launchers keep showing the old icon for a minute after updating; restarting the phone or the launcher refreshes it.
