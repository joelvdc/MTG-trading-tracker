# 1.64: deaths, sacrifice outlets and -1/-1 counters in the win turn
- **Creatures dying:** "whenever a creature (you control) dies" now drains and draws (Blood Artist, The Meathook Massacre, Vein Ripper, Syr Konrad, Midnight Reaper…). Before, only one rare wording counted, and a death that draws a card was taken as a free card every turn.
- **Free sacrifice outlets:** Ashnod's Altar, Carrion Feeder, Warren Soultrader and Yawgmoth are used after combat when what the deaths set off is worth more than the creatures, or when the drains finish the opponents.
- **-1/-1 counters:** blight, Channeler Initiate, Harbinger of Night, Soul Snuffers, Skinrender, Carnifex Demon and the like put them on creatures. Creatures now have toughness and can die to them. Counters on a "target creature" go on an opponent's creature from turn 4, as for the combos.
- **Their payoffs:** Nest of Scarabs, Hapatra, Obelisk Spider, Flourishing Defenses, Blowfly Infestation, The Scorpion God, and Auntie Ool (a card when the counters go on your creature, 1 life when they go on an opponent's; before, a card every turn). All Will Be One deals damage for every counter you put (also +1/+1, loyalty and poison).
- **Also read now:** Animate Dead (before, taken as an aura), "loses 1 life for each Shrine you control" in tap abilities, amass (Dreadhorde Invasion), Mana Geyser (half the opponents' lands taken as tapped), Sunbird's Invocation, and Approach of the Second Sun (the second one cast from your hand wins).
- Win turns are worked out again once. On the 19 "Ready to Play" decks, The Council of Four wins a turn sooner (T10). Smasher LCP, Baba and Poly Jace win a few games per hundred sooner. Auntie Plague and Cult of Athreos win a few later by turn 10 and 11: their free card every turn is gone. Without it, the new rules make both faster. The turn shown stays the same for every other deck.
