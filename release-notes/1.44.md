# 1.44: deck stats and the deck's tokens
- **Stats on deck pages:** a folding "Stats" section above the cards with the **mana curve** (lands left out, with the average), the **mana cost** (the coloured symbols in the cards' costs) and the **mana production**, both as one bar split by colour like Archidekt's, with counts and percentages.
- **Mana production counts sources:** each card counts once for every colour it can make, however much (Twilight Mire is one black and one green source, Sol Ring one colourless). Fetch lands count for the colours of the lands they can find in the deck; Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Arcane Signet and other "any colour" cards count only for the commander's colours; ramp spells like Farseek don't count. Double-faced cards with a land on either face are lands.
- **Lands only and Colourless:** two switches right next to the production bar. Tap a colour to see the cards counted, and what each fetch land finds.
- **Tokens:** a folding "Tokens" section with a picture of every token (and helper cards like The Monarch and Poison Counter) the deck makes, and the cards that make it. Tap a token to see it large and swipe through its other printings (needs internet).
- **First start:** the app looks up the mana your decks' cards make, their rules text and tokens once (when a deck page is opened online), then the stats work offline. No sync change: 1.44 keeps syncing with 1.43.

`MTG-Trader-1.44.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.44-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
