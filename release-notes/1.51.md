# 1.51: pause a game and continue it on any phone, the table lined up on every phone
- **Pause a game and pick it up later, on this phone or your other one.** In the game's history (the clock button in the middle bar), **Pause game and leave** stops the clock, closes the tracker and puts the game with the paused games. They sync through Nextcloud, so both phones list them on the Games tab under **Paused games**, with the date and time, the players, the round and the time played. **Continue** carries on where you left off (and takes the game off the list on both phones); ⋮ → **Drop it** removes it. Several games can be paused at once; continuing one while another game is running pauses that one first.
- **Name, life total and commander damage lined up again on narrower phones.** On some phones (a Samsung A37, for one) the 4-player table put the commander damage grid beside the total instead of under it, so the name, total and grid were no longer in one line. They are now, as in Lifetap.
- **The end seats of a 6-player table:** the commander damage grid now sits right next to the life total instead of out by the +, and the total stays in the middle of the panel.
- **A settings button in the middle bar:** the circle with the number of players is now a ⚙ that opens Game settings.
- Undo last and Pause clock in the game's history are now two equal buttons, with Pause game and leave under them.
- **Update both phones:** the sync file has a new format for the paused games. A phone still on 1.50 or older stops syncing and asks to be updated; its changes wait on the phone and sync after the update.

`MTG-Trader-1.51.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.51-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
