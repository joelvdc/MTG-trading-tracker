# 1.27: signed and altered cards
- **Signed and Altered checkboxes** on the card page. Signed or altered copies are a stack of their own, like etched foils, with a SIGNED or ALTERED tag in the collection. Ticking one on a stack of several copies asks how many: 4 Brainstorms with 1 signed become a stack of 3 and a signed one.
- **Filter:** "Signed or altered" in the filter (Signed, Altered, Neither); typing "signed" or "altered" in the search field works too.
- **Everywhere your cards go:** CSV export and import have "Signed" and "Altered" columns (ManaBox's "Altered" column is read too), CardTrader order imports mark signed and altered cards, and backups keep them.
- **Archidekt:** signed and altered copies are separate entries with a "Signed" or "Altered" label. These two labels sync both ways, whatever your binder label setting.
- **Trade binder:** signed and altered copies are never suggested for trading.
- **Update all your phones:** Nextcloud sync has a new format so older versions can't lose the marks. A phone still on 1.26 or older stops syncing and asks to be updated (its changes wait and sync afterwards), and an updated phone tells you when it sees one.

`MTG-Trader-1.27.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.27-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
