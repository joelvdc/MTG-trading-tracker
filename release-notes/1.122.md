# 1.122: Combos on the deck page, win turns worked out by themselves

- The deck page has a **Combos** section (folded until opened): every combo in the deck, those that count for the
  bracket first, each with Commander Spellbook's tag ("Spicy"), what it does and a "Spellbook" link. A card's own
  combos also show the tag now.
- Win turn: worked out once when a deck's page opens. Editing the deck no longer starts it again: the "Wins by" number
  greys out and a ↻ button works it out for the deck as it is now; reopening the page does the same. Leaving the page
  stops a run that is still going.
- Opening the Decks tab rates decks whose rating is out of date, then works out every win turn that is missing or out
  of date (after an update, a reloaded list or an edit), one deck at a time; it can be stopped. "Update all decks"
  does it too. The deck lists show the saved win turns straight after the app starts.
