# 1.63: battles, planeswalker ultimates and proliferate in the win turn
- **Battles:** a Siege's "when this Siege enters" now counts. Invasion of Zendikar puts two basic lands onto the battlefield (before, it was taken for a creature that taps for mana), and Invasion of Ikoria brings out the best non-Human creature with mana value X or less from your library or graveyard, X being the mana left. Battles aren't attacked to flip them.
- **Planeswalker ultimates:** used once the loyalty is there, when they do something in a game without opponents' creatures: Elspeth, Sun's Champion's +2/+2 emblem, Lord Windgrace's six Cat Warriors, Vraska, Betrayal's Sting's poison. Before, abilities costing −6 or more were never used.
- **Proliferate:** adds a loyalty counter to each of your planeswalkers, a +1/+1 counter to each creature that has some, and a poison counter to each opponent who has some (Vraska, Evolution Sage, Agent Frank Horrigan, Contagion Clasp, Grim Affliction, Atomize…).
- **Poison:** ten poison counters knock a player out, so Vraska's −9 followed by a proliferate wins against that player.
- Win turns are worked out again once. On the 19 "Ready to Play" decks the turn stays the same; Auntie Plague, Poly Jace and Taranika band win a few games per hundred sooner.
