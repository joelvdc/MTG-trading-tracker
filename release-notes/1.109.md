# 1.109: The trade binder leaves your Decks binder alone
- **Trade binder after a sort**: spare copies now come from Unsorted first, then your other binders, then your sort levels from the lowest (e.g. Bulk) up to the best, and from the Decks binder only as a last resort. Before, it could take a deck's copy out of the Decks binder while an identical spare sat in a level. Phone and website.
