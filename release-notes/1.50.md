# 1.50: the ghost keeps clear of the +, low life from a quarter
- **The low-life ghost no longer floats over the +.** It now rises in the empty space between the life total and the +, clear of the name, the total and the commander damage grid. Where the grid sits beside the total (the seats at the ends of a 5–6 player table), it rises on the − side instead.
- **Low life starts at a quarter of the starting life:** the ghost, dark edge or cracks now show at 10 life or less when you start at 40 (it was 9), and at 5 or less when you start at 20.

`MTG-Trader-1.50.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.50-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
