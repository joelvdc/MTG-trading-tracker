# 1.86: recommendations to the maybeboard and wishlist, your own category order
- **Recommendations to the maybeboard.** Tapping a recommended card now offers **Maybeboard** next to **Add to deck**. Decks from Archidekt stay read only, but get a maybeboard of their own in the app and on the website: cards you put there stay when you reload the deck (Archidekt doesn't see them), and you can remove them again.
- **Your own category order.** On the website you can drag a deck's categories into the order you like (decks from Archidekt too); the app shows them in that order.
- **Website:** recommendations have **+ Deck**, **+ Maybe** and **☆ wishlist** buttons on each card, a green corner (✓ and how many) on cards you already own, one price per card, "New" beside the name, small/medium/large pictures (also in the collection) and a working Card on hover. On the deck page the categories no longer jump when you fold one, cards in your own decks can be dragged to another category, the hover picture stays when you flip a card, and the bracket buttons sit beside the scores.
- **Update every phone:** this version changes the sync format, so a phone on an older version pauses syncing until it's updated.
