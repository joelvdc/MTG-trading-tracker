# 1.81: card text and rulings, prices from the website, website categories
- **Card text and rulings:** a card's window (collection, deck and recommendations) now shows the card's text under the picture: every face with its name, mana cost, type line, rules text with mana symbols, and power/toughness or loyalty. Below it, two tabs: **Versions** (everything the window showed before) and **Rulings** (where the card is legal, banned or restricted, and Scryfall's rulings with their dates). The text and rulings are fetched the first time you open a card and kept on the phone, so they work offline afterwards.
- **Prices from the website:** phones logged in to an MTG Trader account now download the website's nightly copy of Cardmarket's prices (about 2 MB) instead of Cardmarket's own file (about 27 MB), with the exchange rates included. If the website can't be reached, the app downloads from Cardmarket as before. Settings → Cardmarket price data shows where the prices came from. Card Kingdom and TCGplayer prices are downloaded as before. Phones that sync with Nextcloud are unchanged.
- **Auto categories from the website:** "Auto categories for new cards" now uses the website's own categories (Ramp, Removal, Draw…, built from Scryfall's card tags), so the app and the website agree. The website builds its list for the first time on the night after this release; until then new cards get no automatic category.
- **Fixed:** a signed (or altered) pile and a plain pile of the same card in the same binder were merged into one at sync. They now stay apart.
- **Website** (no app update needed): import decks from Archidekt by link or username, reload a decklist, score again on Commander Salt, "Update all decks"; the win turn of every deck is worked out by the website itself, and again only when a decklist changes; owned icons on the deck page (a tick for the printing you own, an amber ring when you own another version, an empty ring when you own none) with a Collection tracker box; tokens in their own section; and a new card window on the deck page: card text, a Rulings tab, how many copies you own and where, which other decks have the card, and on local decks "Change printing" with the printings you own first.
