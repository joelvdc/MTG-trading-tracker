# 1.62: win turn timing, rituals, land drops and modes
- **When triggers go:** "at the beginning of combat" and "at the beginning of your end step" triggers now also go the turn the card comes in, and upkeep triggers wait for the next upkeep (before, both went right away). "At the beginning of each end step" and "each combat" count too, unless they have a condition. Tokens that have or gain haste attack the turn they're made (Legion Warboss).
- **Rituals:** Dark Ritual, Cabal Ritual, Seething Song and the like are cast when their mana pays for something more, or for spell triggers.
- **Mana for each of a kind:** Gaea's Cradle, Cabal Coffers and Priest of Titania now count; Elvish Archdruid no longer taps for one extra or on the turn it comes in.
- **Cost reducers:** "Spells you cast cost {1} less", commander spells, and spells cast from anywhere other than your hand (the command zone, the graveyard).
- **Extra land drops:** Azusa, Exploration, Explore and the like; a fetch land sets off landfall twice (itself and the land it finds).
- **Modes:** "choose one" and "choose two" cards now count only their best modes (before, every mode added up: Boros Charm both dealt 4 and gave double strike). "Choose one or more" still counts them all.
- **Spell // land cards** (Malakir Rebirth, Shatterskull Smashing) are played as lands, and cast as the spell once you have three lands out and five counting your hand.
- Win turns are worked out again once. On the 19 "Ready to Play" decks the turn stays the same; a few games per hundred move either way.
