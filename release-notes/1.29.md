# 1.29: TCGplayer and Card Kingdom prices
- **Settings → Price source:** value your cards with Cardmarket (as before), TCGplayer or Card Kingdom. The collection total, sorting, the value filter, trades and the trade binder all follow it. Card Kingdom's price follows each copy's condition (NM, EX, VG, G). A card the chosen source has no price for uses Cardmarket's, marked "≈".
- **Compare them:** the collection value screen lists all sources side by side, plus what Card Kingdom would pay for your cards (its buylist). Tap one to see its total and chart; each source's chart fills in from today.
- **Stats:** "Value by price source" (for the whole collection or one binder) and "Europe or the US?", the cards whose price differs most between Cardmarket and TCGplayer.
- **Card page:** "Prices elsewhere" shows TCGplayer's and Card Kingdom's price for that copy, with links to buy there.
- TCGplayer's prices come with Scryfall's card data; Card Kingdom's from its daily price list (about 10 MB). They download with the Cardmarket prices, follow the Wi-Fi setting, and are converted from dollars with the daily exchange rates.

`MTG-Trader-1.29.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.29-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
