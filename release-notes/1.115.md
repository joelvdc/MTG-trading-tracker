# 1.115: Salt sum on every deck, Bracket from setting

- Every deck shows a salt sum like Archidekt's: EDHREC's salt of every card, lands and commander included. It replaces
  Commander Salt's percent on the deck page, the power card, the cards of all decks and the printed cards, with the
  five saltiest cards and a level (mild under 30, moderate under 45, high).
- New setting Decks → Bracket from: Commander Salt, or Estimated by the app for Archidekt decks too (local decks are
  always estimated). Synced with the website.
- Estimated brackets: land tutors (Evolving Wilds, fetch lands and the like) no longer count as tutors.
- Estimated decks say "Estimated by the app" instead of "Scored by Commander Salt", and show one bracket.
- Every estimated deck is rated again once with the new rules.
