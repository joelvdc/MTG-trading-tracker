# 1.26: import CardTrader orders
- **Import a CardTrader order:** ⋮ → Import CardTrader order (Excel) and pick the order file you downloaded from CardTrader (.xls or .xlsx). The app finds each card on Scryfall, including tokens, The List and collector's versions, and shows the whole order before adding anything.
- **You choose what goes in:** untick any card, and leave out tokens and basic lands with two switches (both on to start with). Cards found by name only are marked "Check the printing", and cards that weren't found say "Find the card"; tap either to pick the right printing.
- **Into a binder of your choice:** pick an existing binder or make a new one, suggested as "CardTrader" plus the order date. Cards keep their finish, condition (Slightly Played becomes EX, Moderately Played LP…), language and the price you paid per copy.
- Signed or altered cards are added as normal copies; the summary afterwards tells you how many there were. A restore point is made before every import.

`MTG-Trader-1.26.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.26-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
