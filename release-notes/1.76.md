# 1.76: going easy on Archidekt
- **A safe pace:** every request to Archidekt (decks, your collection, deck edits) now waits until 1.5 seconds after the previous one, so the app never sends more than 40 a minute, Archidekt's limit. A big sync takes longer; its progress shows the time left.
- **Automatic Archidekt sync** on opening the app now runs at most every 6 hours (was hourly). Leaving the app still sends your changes, only when cards changed.
- **Logging in to Archidekt** says plainly that it's unofficial: the app uses your account through the website's own connection, at your own risk, and your password goes only to Archidekt.
- **Switches:** the app reads `remote-switches.json` from its GitHub page once a day, so Archidekt login and sync (or deck reading) can be switched off on every phone at once if ever needed, with a message in the app. Edits then wait on the phone.
