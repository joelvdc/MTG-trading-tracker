# 1.54: the win turn at 4 in 10 games, and a simulator that reads Shrines, Gates, Maze's End and sacrifice decks
- **Bracket cards show the turn a deck wins by in 4 of 10 games** (it was 5 in 10): the small tile on the list of all decks' bracket cards (still "Wins by") and the full card's Speed section.
- **Winning the game** (deck page → Stats) highlights two turns: the first turn the deck can win (1% of games or more) in **green**, and 4 in 10 games in **gold**, in the headline and in the row of turns. The row now starts at the first win, so early combo wins are in view. The bracket card's Speed section does the same.
- **The simulator reads much more**:
  - effects that count permanents: "for each Shrine you control", "where X is the number of Gates…" (damage, drain, tokens, cards, +1/+1 counters, Gatebreaker Ram);
  - triggers when a kind of permanent enters ("whenever another Shrine you control enters"); Go-Shintai's Shrine tokens count as Shrines; Sanctum of All and Hei Bai put Shrines onto the battlefield;
  - **Maze's End now wins** (ten Gates with different names): it's played early and used every turn it can, Gate searches bring real Gates, Sylvan Scrying finds Maze's End, and Amulet of Vigor, Spelunking and Gond Gate let lands enter untapped. Crackling Perimeter, Basilisk Gate and Sage of the Maze count too. Maze's End wins are listed on their own;
  - sacrifice decks like Baba: her ability the turn she comes in (Thousand-Year Elixir, Tyvar, Greaves), again with untaps (Instill Energy, Nature's Chosen, Patriar's Seal) or Marvin, Liquimetal Coating, and the payoffs (Marionette Master, Titania, Baloth Prime, Mazirek, lands that leave tokens);
  - X spells that drain each opponent (Exsanguinate, Jaya's Immolating Inferno) with the mana left, and Double Vision copying them.
- Fixed: Hour of Promise no longer makes Zombies without three Deserts.
- **Your decks (4 in 10 games by):** Shrine Gate Win turn 15 → **10** (Maze's End wins 1 in 5 games), Baba 14 → **13**, Smasher LCP stays at 13 (but 9 in 10 by turn 15 now); the other decks don't change.
- Every deck's win turn is worked out again once (a few seconds per deck).

`MTG-Trader-1.54.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.54-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
