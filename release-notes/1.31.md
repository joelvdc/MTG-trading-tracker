# 1.31: price sources downloaded once
- **No more double download:** opening the app could download TCGplayer's and Card Kingdom's prices twice, because the app start, the "back online" check and the background update each decided "it's due" before waiting for their turn. The check now happens when the turn comes, so the later ones see the fresh prices and skip, like Cardmarket's.

`MTG-Trader-1.31.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.31-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
