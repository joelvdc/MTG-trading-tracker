# 1.34: deck pictures offline, add to the wishlist offline
- **Deck covers show offline:** a deck's cover (its Archidekt art or its commander's art) used to disappear offline an hour or a day after it was last seen, because Archidekt only lets pictures be kept that long. Pictures the app has saved now always show, online or offline.
- **Deck and wishlist pictures saved ahead:** on Wi-Fi, the app saves the pictures of every deck (its cards, in the printings it shows, and its cover) and of the wishlist, even ones you never opened, so they show without internet. Turn it off in Settings → "Save deck and wishlist pictures for offline use". About 10–15 MB per deck; saved pictures take at most 600 MB.
- **Add to the wishlist offline:** without internet, "Add card" on the wishlist suggests names from Cardmarket's card list already on the phone. The card goes on the wishlist by name (any printing will do), marked "Details follow when you're online"; its picture, set and prices are filled in by themselves once the phone is back online.
- **Both phones:** nothing changes in the database or the sync file. A phone still on an older version shows a card added offline by name only until this phone fills it in.

`MTG-Trader-1.34.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.34-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
