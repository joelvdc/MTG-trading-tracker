# 1.100: MDFC lands in the Land count, precons on the website
- **Land heading counts MDFCs**: on a deck's page the Land section now reads like Archidekt's, e.g. "Land (37, 40 with MDFCs)": the modal double-faced cards with a land on the back (Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs) are counted in brackets, while they stay in their own category.
- **Lands always go in Land**: new cards placed "in their auto category" never land in Tutor or Ramp any more just because Scryfall tags them so (Evolving Wilds was a "tutor"). This comes through the website's categories table, rebuilt on 2026-11-18. Decks you already have keep their placement until you use "Put every card in its auto category".
- **On the website**: a new top bar with an **Explore** menu, and **Commander precons**: every Commander precon as picture tiles with search and colour/year filters; open one to see it with the auto categories, its simulated win turn and power level and how many of its cards you own, then **Make it my deck**, **Add its cards to my collection** or **Copy decklist**. Orders, Settings and Log out moved to the menu under your name.
- No sync change: phones on 1.97 and later keep syncing.
