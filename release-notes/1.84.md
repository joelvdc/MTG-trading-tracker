# 1.84: open a recommended card, and list-or-images recommendations
- **Tap a recommended card to open its window:** in a deck's recommendations (EDHREC or recommander.cards), tapping a card opens its window with the picture, what the source says about it (share, synergy, score), the card text and rulings, the price and shop links (EDHREC, Cardmarket, CardTrader) — and **which decks play the card and how many copies you own**.
- **Recommendations as list or images:** the List/Images toggle (next to the source) shows the recommendations as rows or as a grid of pictures; the choice is remembered. In Images, a tap opens the card window.
- **On the website** too: tapping a recommended card on a deck page opens the same card window, nearly full-screen like Archidekt's and Moxfield's, with the decks that use it and your copies of it; the recommendations there also have the List/Images toggle, and all sections are open by default so recommander.cards' sections match the app's.
