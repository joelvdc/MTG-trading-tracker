# 1.37: price alerts for wishlist cards, with CardTrader
- **Price alerts:** open a wishlist card and fill in "Notify me at or below". When the card costs that much or less, you get a notification; tapping it opens the card. Leave it empty for no alert.
- **Once per drop:** after a notification the card stays quiet until its price has been above your level again (or you change the level). Ordered cards stay quiet.
- **Cheapest printing:** for "any printing" cards the alert looks at the cheapest printing, and the list shows it under the card: "Low from €41.50 (MH2)".
- **Which price:** the price source from Settings. With Cardmarket you pick its price for alerts in Settings → Wishlist price alerts: lowest listing (the default), trend price, 7-day or 1-day average. The menu explains what each means for an alert.
- **CardTrader:** switch on "Also check CardTrader" and enter your CardTrader API token (it stays on the phone, encrypted, not synced). Each wishlist card then shows CardTrader's cheapest offer and an "Open on CardTrader" link, and alerts count it too. Choose the language and the worst condition that count.
- **CardTrader Zero:** "Only CardTrader Zero offers" is on by default; the cheapest offer from any seller shows next to it. When none of the 25 offers CardTrader shows for a printing is CT Zero, the card says "CT Zero over €…" (a CT Zero copy costs at least that).
- **Checked a few times a day,** after each price update; "Update automatically" must be on. Android 13 and later ask once whether MTG Trader may send notifications.
- **Update both phones:** the alert levels sync, so the sync file (and backups) went to a new version. A phone still on 1.36 or older stops syncing and asks to be updated.

`MTG-Trader-1.37.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.37-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
