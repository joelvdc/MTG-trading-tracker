# 1.91: wishlist share link, and trades, saved views and price charts on the website
- **Share wishlist link** (Wishlist → menu): a read-only web page with the cards you still want and their prices, to send to a friend or a shop. No notes, alerts or orders are shown. Needs the website account (Settings → Website). The old item is now called "Share the wishlist as text".
- **About** (Settings) lists every data source the app uses, the Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy wording, and where to find the privacy and attribution pages.
- **Website** (mtgtrader.vercel.app):
  - **Trades** can be made, edited, completed (the collection is updated: received cards go into the binder you choose, given cards come out the way the app takes them), undone, deleted (with Undo) and exported as CSV, following the app's rules. The phones pick up the changes at their next sync.
  - **Saved views** on the Collection page: keep a binder, search, filter, sort, columns, grouping and layout under a name, kept with your account, so every computer has them.
  - **Group printings** in the list view: one line per card name, with the printings folded underneath.
  - **Spare filter** under Decks: "Only spare copies (you own more than your decks use)", beside "Only cards no deck uses".
  - **Price chart** in the collection card window (1M/3M/6M/1Y), from the daily prices the website saves every night. It is kept for a year: daily for three months, then one price a week. It starts with only the days saved so far.
  - **Share link for the wishlist**, like the app's.
  - A **footer** with the data sources and the Fan Content Policy, plus **Privacy** and **Legal & attribution** pages; Contact goes to the project's GitHub issues page.
