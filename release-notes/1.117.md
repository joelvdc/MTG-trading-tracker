# 1.117: Commander Spock's free spells in the win turn

- The win turn now plays Commander Spock's "enters or attacks" ability as written: the top card is cast for free when
  its mana value is at most your number of differently named lands, a land goes onto the battlefield tapped, and only
  otherwise is the card drawn. Before, it counted as drawing a card, so Spock decks showed a later win turn.
- Win turns are worked out again once, on the phone and on the website.
