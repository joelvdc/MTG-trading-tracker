# 1.103: Price paid for scanned cards, and which order a card came from
- **Price paid in the Scan tab**: tap a scanned card and fill in "Purchase price per copy" (in euros, kroner or dollars). The scan list shows it under the card's value. When you send the scans to a binder, the price goes along. If the card joins a stack you already own, the stack's price paid becomes the average of all copies. Undo puts everything back as it was. Sending scans to a trade or a deck leaves the price behind.
- **Ordered**: a card's page in your collection now lists the orders that card came in, for example "12 Mar 2026 · Cardmarket shipment 1234567 · 2× · €1.20 each". Tap a line to open that order. Precons you added show as "Precon: <deck>". Cardmarket's monthly "Purchased Articles" file doesn't say whether a card was foil, so its lines say "finish unknown". It works per printing: it can't tell which physical copy in a stack came from which order. Only imported orders show up.
- **On the website (live with this release)**: the same two things. A scanned card's window has "Paid per copy", and "To binder" takes the price along. The collection's card window has an "Ordered" list whose links open the order on the Orders page.
- **Sync change**: scanned cards now sync their price, so the sync format goes to 16. A phone still on 1.102 or older pauses syncing and asks to be updated; update both phones.
