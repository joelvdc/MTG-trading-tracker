# 1.43: one line per card and mana costs on deck pages
- **Mana costs on deck pages:** every card shows its mana cost in the usual symbols (hybrid and Phyrexian mana too; both faces of double-faced cards), in both views and in the card's window. The set and number moved to the card's window (tap a card).
- **Compact view, one line per card:** amount, name, mana cost and price on a single line, like Archidekt's list. After the name: ✦ for foil, **+** for added in this app, and the bracket labels: GC (game changer), COMBO, EXTRA TURN and LAND DENIAL in red, TUTOR and FAST MANA in grey.
- **Long names come first:** when a name and its labels don't fit, the labels shorten (TURN, LD, FAST) and then become small dots (red or grey); the name is only cut off with "…" when even that isn't enough. Tap the card to see its labels in full.
- **Section headings show their value:** what the cards in each category (or card type) are worth, next to the count.
- **Picture view:** the mana cost and the labels are on the line under the name.
- **First start:** the app looks up the mana costs of your decks' cards once (about a minute, when online), then they work offline. No sync change: 1.43 keeps syncing with 1.42.

`MTG-Trader-1.43.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.43-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
