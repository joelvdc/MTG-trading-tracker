# 1.72: arrange the table by dragging seats
- **The seats as a table:** the New game screen draws the players where they really sit around the phone, instead of a list with arrows. It's drawn as you see it from your chair with the phone tilted left; the turn button next to "Seats" switches to tilted right or upright (remembered, and on your other phone too).
- **Drag to swap:** hold a seat and drag it onto another, and the two players trade places. Tap a seat to pick the player and deck, as before.
- **Also during a game:** Game settings shows the same table. Swapping seats mid-game moves everything with the player: life, commander damage (taken and dealt), poison, counters, whose turn it is, the first player, the monarch and the initiative. Undo puts them back, and a rematch keeps the new seating.
