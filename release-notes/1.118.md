# 1.118: See which deck cards you own

- New on the deck page's ⋮ menu: **Show cards I own**. When it's on, cards you own in the deck's printing get a green
  background and cards you own in another printing (or too few copies of) get a blue one. Basic lands stay plain.
- It compares with the printing the deck lists on Archidekt, the same way as the website, also when "Show the printings
  I own" puts your own copy on the line.
- Website: the deck list's collection dots are now green (that printing), blue (another printing) and a grey ring (not
  owned), without ticks.
