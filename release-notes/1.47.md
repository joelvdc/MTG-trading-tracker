# 1.47: life tracker and game records, Manage categories, and deck page fixes
- **Games tab with a life tracker:** the phone lies in the middle of the table, one panel per player (2 to 6), each facing its player with their commander's picture (or a plain colour). Tap to change life, hold for 10; commander damage, poison, commander tax and extra counters (energy, experience, rad, storm, monarch, initiative, day/night or your own). At 0 life, 10 poison or 21 commander damage, "Dead?" asks; yes shows a skull. Low life gets a ghost (or another animation, Settings → Life tracker).
- **Turns and clock (optional):** whose turn it is, the round, the turn's and the game's time, pause and undo; who starts can be picked at random.
- **Game records and stats:** finished games are kept (and synced): who won, places, how, length and turns. Stats per deck (win rate, average length, the turn it usually wins on, its bracket next to its opponents'), per player and head to head; each deck's page has a Games section. Friends and their decks are added once and kept. Export the history as a CSV file; add a past game by hand.
- **Settings moved:** behind the ⚙ at the top of every tab, to make room for Games.
- **Manage categories:** in the deck's ⋮ menu, in place of "Add a category…": add, rename, delete and switch off categories. Switched-off categories leave the move lists, and their cards go to a folded "Hidden categories" section. Empty categories are no longer shown on the deck page. Adding, renaming and deleting now happen on Archidekt too.
- **Fixes:** dragging a card to another category works again; long menus and lists no longer run under the phone's buttons at the bottom; the card window's category opens a list from the bottom and its Deck/Maybeboard switch fits with larger text; the "Casting the commander" odds no longer start over when scrolled past. The Decks tab's "Local" section no longer says "on this phone": local decks have always synced between your phones with Nextcloud sync.
- **Update both phones:** the sync file changes with this version (games), so both phones need 1.47 before they sync again.

`MTG-Trader-1.47.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.47-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
