# 1.40: type a price in the currency you paid in
- **A currency button in every price field:** "€ ▾", "kr. ▾" or "$ ▾" at the end of the field. Type the amount as you paid it, e.g. 10 in euros while the app shows kroner; no converting by hand.
- **It says what it comes to:** a price typed in another currency than the one shown gets a line under it, e.g. "≈ 74,61 kr.". The app converts it with the day's exchange rate.
- **Everywhere you type a price:** a card's purchase price (its page and the card window), the agreed price in a trade, "Edit condition, language, price…" for selected cards, "Add to collection" from the wishlist, what a precon cost, and the alert level on a wishlist card.
- **It remembers your choice:** a new price field starts on the currency you picked last (kept on each phone), or on the one the app shows until you pick one.
- **Nothing else changes:** prices already saved show as before, and the two phones keep syncing with 1.39.

`MTG-Trader-1.40.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.40-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
