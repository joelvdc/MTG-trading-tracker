# 1.71: smarter land drops in the commander and win-turn odds
- **Colourless lands aren't a colour:** the odds no longer play Kessig Wolf Run, Bonders' Enclave and the like early as if {C} were a missing colour. A Forest or Mountain goes down instead.
- **Tapped lands first when the mana isn't needed:** when an untapped land's mana wouldn't be used that turn (turn 1 mostly, or a turn with nothing to cast), a land that enters tapped is played, the way players do. Lands that could come in untapped later wait.
- **"Enters tapped unless …" lands check their condition:** slow lands (Rockfall Vale), fast lands (Copperline Gorge), check lands (Rootbound Crag), battle lands (Cinder Glade), Game Trail's reveal and Den of the Bugbear. Shock lands and others that cost life come in untapped.
- For Ruby, Daring Tracker: casting Ruby on turn 2 goes from 66% to 90%. Turn 1 now shows fewer colours, because tapped duals go down first.
- Saved win turns are worked out again once.
