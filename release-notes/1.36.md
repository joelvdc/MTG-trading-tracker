# 1.36: tick "Ordered" right in the wishlist
- **A box on every wishlist card:** each card on the wishlist has an "Ordered" checkbox on the right. One tap marks it as ordered, without opening the card; "Undo" at the bottom takes back a mis-tap. The checkbox in the card's window is still there too.
- **Ordered cards fade:** a ticked card is shown faded (all but its box), so what you still have to buy stands out.
- **In every view:** the box shows in the normal list, the compact list and the grid (in the picture's corner).
- **Shorter date:** the label under an ordered card says "📦 ordered Oct 15"; the year only shows for orders from an earlier year.
- **Both phones:** nothing changes in the database or the sync file; 1.35 and 1.36 sync with each other.

`MTG-Trader-1.36.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.36-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
