# 1.59: Lifetap's dice
- **Dice as in Lifetap:** the Dice page draws its dice with Dicefont, the icon font Lifetap uses: the d8, d10, d12 and d20 now show all their edges, the coin is a circle with a 2, and the custom die says "Set 100" above Roll.
- **A roll shows the die:** the result appears in a big box over the Dice page, which stays behind it: the dots for a d6, the number on the die for the others, a coin with H or T. **Again** sits under it; tap outside the box to go back to the dice. One die only, as in Lifetap (it no longer shows twice, upside down for the other side).
- **Quick roll, as in Lifetap:** hold the die in the middle bar: the table darkens and a ring of choices glides out around it (d4 to d20, the coin, random opponent, random player). Slide your finger to one (it grows and turns blue, with a short buzz) and let go to roll it on the table. Let go anywhere else and nothing happens. A short tap still opens the Dice page.
- **Random opponent asks who you are:** every panel says "I'm …"; the player asking taps their own, and the pick runs around the table among the others still in the game. The back button cancels.
