# 1.85: decks from Archidekt are read only, group and sort decks by mana value
- **Decks from Archidekt are read only, in the app too.** The deck page's **Add card** button, the Scan tab's **To a deck** and **Add to deck** in Recommendations now only offer your local decks. To add cards to a deck from Archidekt, edit it on archidekt.com and reload it, or make a local copy (deck ⋮ → **Make it a local deck**). Cards you added in the app before stay under "Added in app" and can still be removed.
- **Group and sort a deck's cards** (deck ⋮ → **Group and sort…**): group by category, card type, mana value or colour, and sort each section by mana value or name, optionally descending, like the website's deck page. The choice is remembered on the phone.
