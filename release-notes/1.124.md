# 1.124: Deck snapshots, right-click card menu, playtester power and toughness, win turn fixes

- **Deck snapshots**, in the app (deck menu → Snapshots) and on the website (More → Snapshots): save the whole list
  under a name, with the bracket, win turn and price of that moment; compare two snapshots, or one with the deck as
  it is now (bracket, win turn, price, cards added and removed); rename, delete, or copy one as a new local deck. Up
  to 25 per deck, synced between phones and the website.
- **Right-click a card** on a deck page of the website for a menu like Archidekt's: open details, copy the card name,
  Scryfall, EDHREC, Cardmarket and CardTrader, and on your own local decks +1/−1 copy, change printing, make
  commander, maybeboard, move to category, select and remove. Shared decks get the copy and links part.
- **Playtester:** Power ›, Toughness › and +X/+X › in a battlefield card's menu (also for a selection), with a badge
  showing the card's power and toughness (green when bigger, red when smaller). The change goes when the card leaves
  the battlefield.
- **Win turn** (the simulator, version 17) now plays: Lander tokens (used for a basic land), Station spacecraft
  (Exploration Broodship's extra land drop, Starship Enterprise's charge counters, becoming creatures), U.S.S.
  Farragut's creature from the hand, creature lands such as Mutavault, landfall counters (Amanda Grayson) and mana
  (Lotus Cobra), one mode of a "choose one" landfall trigger (Felidar Retreat), Planar Genesis's land, Will of the
  Abzan's drain on each opponent, Saw in Half's two copies and Coiling Rebirth's gift copy. Emergency Eject no longer counts as ramp. Win turns are worked out again for every deck.
- Phones still on 1.123 or older stop syncing until they are updated (the sync file's format is now 17), so a
  snapshot is never lost on the way.
