# 1.49: a slower random pick, your own commander's damage, the grid centred
- **Random player, random opponent and who starts:** the run around the table now takes about 3 seconds, 2 seconds more than before, and slows down like a wheel before it stops on the player.
- **Commander damage from your own commander** (when another player controls it and attacks you): your own box on the player's sheet counts like the others (tap +1, hold for − and + and partners). On the panel your box says "me" until it has damage, then shows the number. It takes life and counts towards 21 like any other, and the history says "from their own …".
- **The commander damage mini-grid is centred** under the life total in a light frame, as in Lifetap. Counter badges (tax, poison, energy…) hang beside it without pushing it off the middle.

`MTG-Trader-1.49.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.49-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
