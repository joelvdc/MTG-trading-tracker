# 1.39: wishlist cards into the collection, orders against the wishlist, arranging binders
- **Swipe right moves a wishlist card one step on:** To buy → Ordered → Received → the collection. Swipe left still deletes; every swipe can be undone.
- **Received cards into the collection:** swiped right in Received, a window asks what the app can't know: the printing (confirm it for "any printing" cards, pick it for cards added offline), finish, condition, language, copies, binder and what you paid. The card then leaves the wishlist.
- **Same version already in your collection?** The copies join that stack, and its purchase price becomes the average of all copies.
- **Imported an order?** After adding an order (or a checked package), the app lists the order's cards that are on your wishlist: mark them as received (preselected), take them off, or leave them. Received this way, a card shows the order ("✓ received Oct 20 · CardTrader order #12345") and is never added to the collection twice: swiping it right just takes it off the wishlist.
- **A safety net:** if the app sees you got a copy since adding a card (e.g. from an order imported before 1.39), the "Add to collection" window warns you and offers "Just take it off the wishlist".
- **Arrange binders:** ⋮ → Arrange binders… moves Unsorted, the Wishlist and each binder up or down. The binder bar and the binder lists follow that order, on both phones.
- **Alert levels keep their currency:** 300 kr. now stays 300 kr. instead of drifting with the daily exchange rate. Levels set before 1.39 were kept in euros: type them again once.
- **Update both phones:** the sync file (and backups) went to a new version. A phone still on 1.38 or older stops syncing and asks to be updated.

`MTG-Trader-1.39.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.39-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
