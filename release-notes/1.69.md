# 1.69: the win turn reads more lands, abilities and finishers
- **Lands that make more mana:** Lotus Field makes three mana (and sacrifices two lands, tapped for mana first), the depletion lands (Saprazzan Skerry, Peat Bog, Hickory Woodlot…) make two for two turns, Ancient Tomb two, and Crypt of Agadeem one black mana for each black creature card in your graveyard. This counts for casting the commander too. Lands whose mana has a condition (Temple of the False God) still count as one.
- **Abilities with a sacrifice or blight cost:** Cauldron of Essence, Alquist Proft (draw X with the mana left), Fain, the Broker, Dawnhand Dissident, Technodrome and the like are used when what they do is worth more than what they cost.
- **Finishers:** Akroma's Will gives every attacker double strike, Jeska's Will adds mana (an opponent's hand taken as five cards) and plays two cards, Soul Immolation blights your toughest creature for that much damage to each opponent, and Mizzix's Mastery recasts instants and sorceries from the graveyard. Storm of Souls now brings creatures back as 1/1s.
- **Sagas, Vehicles, toxic:** Saga chapters happen one a turn (Maximum Carnage's mana and 5 damage, Urza's Saga's Construct and its artifact search), Vehicles attack when a creature can crew them, toxic creatures and Mites give poison counters, and Twenty-Toed Toad wins with twenty cards in hand.
- "Choose one" spells pick double strike over a few damage, and Boros Charm and similar spells read all their modes.
- Saved win turns are worked out again once.
