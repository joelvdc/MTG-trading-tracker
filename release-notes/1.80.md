# 1.80: trade binder rules and value history shared with the website
- **Trade binder rules follow you:** the trade binder's rules (most cards, minimum value, keep one copy, keep the best copy) and the suggestions you declined now sync to your other phones and the website. The website has its own trade binder page (Collection → Trade binder) to accept or decline suggestions and change the rules.
- **Your value history goes to your account once:** after logging in with an MTG Trader account, the phone sends its value history to the account, so the website's new chart (Collection → Stats → Value over time) starts where the phone's does. From then on the website saves the value every night by itself.
- **Website:** price alerts are now also checked every night on the website, against Cardmarket at your alert price type; the ones reached are listed under Wishlist → Alert history. The phones keep their own notifications and history, with CardTrader too.
