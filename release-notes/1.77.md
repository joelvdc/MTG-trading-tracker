# 1.77: widgets, value by set, alert history
- **Home-screen widgets:** "Collection value" shows your collection's value and its change over 7 days; drag it wider for a 7-day chart and the three cards that moved most. "Wishlist" shows the cards closest to their alert price (✓ once reached). Tapping opens the Value screen or the card. They follow the phone's light or dark mode and refresh whenever the app updates prices; they never go online themselves.
- **Value by set:** the Value screen lists your sets by value (the top ten, or all), with each set's share and its recent change. Tap a set to see its cards. It follows the binder and price source chosen on the screen.
- **Price alert history:** each time a wishlist card reaches its alert price, the phone notes when, at what price and where. The last three show in the card's window, all of them under Wishlist ⋮ → Alert history. Kept on each phone for a year.
- **Land drops in the win turn and commander odds:** an untapped land goes down when an X spell or an ability (Kessig Wolf Run, equip) can use its mana; Mines of Moria and The Shire check for a legendary creature, Konstrari Annex for a planeswalker, Dwarven Mine for three other Mountains, Bleeding Woods for a player at 13 life; a ramp spell's basic is the one colour the deck lacks most. Saved win turns are worked out again, so some may move a little.
