# 1.60: Prices keep the currency you typed
- **75 kr. stays 75 kr.:** a purchase price or an agreed trade price typed in kroner or dollars now also remembers the amount as you typed it. While the app shows that currency, it shows exactly that (75,00 kr., not 74,98 kr. a month later), and the price field opens on 75,00 kr. again. In another currency it's converted as before.
- **Still kept in euros:** totals, Paid and worth, profit and loss, trade balances and Archidekt keep using the euros the price was converted to when you typed it, so nothing else changes. Archidekt only takes one number, so it still gets euros; a price changed on Archidekt comes back in euros.
- **Which currency, when stacks join:** when copies paid in one currency join a stack whose price was typed in another (a received wishlist card, an order, a package check, a precon, or purchase prices from orders), the app asks which currency the stack's average price is kept in.
- **Precons:** what you paid for a precon in kroner is split over its cards in kroner too.
- **CSV export:** two new columns, "Purchase price as typed" and "Typed currency", which the import reads back. The euro columns stay as they were.
- **Update both phones:** the Nextcloud sync file has a new format (12). A phone still on an older version stops syncing and asks to be updated, so nothing typed in kroner gets lost.
