# 1.52: when each deck can win, on the deck stats and the bracket card; the turn badge in the name label
- **Winning the game (deck page → Stats):** the turn the deck usually wins by, the turn it has won by in 9 of 10 games, and the share of games won by each turn. The app plays the deck alone 10,000 times against three players at 40 life: it casts its commander, creatures, token makers, equipment, auras and damage cards, puts the equipment and auras on the commander, keeps pump spells for combat and attacks with everything. A game is won when all three players are at 0, when **21 commander damage** has knocked each out, or with a combo that wins on its own (Commander Salt's "wins the game" or "infinite damage" combos that don't need more cards on the battlefield).
- **Read from the cards:** equipment and auras (+N/+N, "for each…", double strike, haste, equip costs, For Mirrodin!, living weapon), pump spells (also +X/+X), double strike, haste, tokens, anthems, card draw (also Sergeant John Benton's "draw that many cards"), drain and burn, and **damage triggers** like Vial Smasher's (casting spells), Purphoros' (creatures entering), landfall, and triggers at the start of your turn or when attacking. **Sacrifice abilities like Baba Lysaga's** count too: Baba is tapped each turn it can, sacrificing the cheapest permanents that cover three card types (Food, Clue and Treasure tokens first, then spare lands; an artifact land counts as two types), and drains and draws; the deck then casts its permanents as fodder. With your decks: Sarge usually wins by turn 8, Cloud and Auntie Plague by turn 11, Smasher LCP by turn 13, Baba by turn 15.
- Under the table: where the damage came from (combat, commander damage, triggers, drain) and how often commander damage knocked a player out; **How?** lists what was counted. Decks that don't win by turn 15 in most games show **T15+** with a note, as their damage likely comes from what the app can't read yet (sacrifice triggers, a commander's own ability). It's a best case (no opponents, removal or blockers), for comparing decks.
- **On the bracket card:** a **Speed** section under the two brackets with the same numbers, and on **Bracket cards of all decks** each small card has a third tile with the turn (**T11**, **T15+**).
- **Life tracker:** the gold turn badge now sits at the start of the player's name label instead of in the corner over the name; tap it to pass the turn. The deck name is shortened when there's no room.
- **Scanner:** the list under the camera now jumps to the newest card each time one is added, so the last card scanned is always in sight at the top.
- The win turns are worked out in the background (a few seconds per deck) and kept on the phone until the deck, its card details or its combos change; nothing new is synced.

`MTG-Trader-1.52.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.52-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
