# 1.46: edit decks, local decks, every combo, and when the deck casts its commander
- **Edit your decks:** your own Archidekt decks (the account logged in under Settings) and local decks can be changed in the app. Drag a card by its handle (⠿) to another category, or press and hold to select cards and move them, put them in the maybeboard or remove them. A category's ⋮ adds a card there, renames or deletes it. Tapping a card shows its category, deck or maybeboard, and copies. Every edit can be undone.
- **Edits go to Archidekt:** changes show at once and are sent to Archidekt in the background. Offline they wait on the phone ("2 edits waiting") and go when you're online again; only the phone that made them sends them. An edit Archidekt refuses is skipped and reported.
- **Auto categories:** new cards go into Archidekt's own auto category (Ramp, Draw, Removal…), or by card type when they have none; "Auto-categorise all cards" in the deck's ⋮ menu re-sorts a deck. A switch in Settings turns it off.
- **Local decks:** New deck makes a deck that lives in the app: empty, from a precon, from scanned cards, as a copy, or from a pasted Moxfield list (Archidekt, Arena and plain lists work too; its tags can become the categories). Any Archidekt deck can be detached into a local deck.
- **Send to Archidekt:** a local deck can become a new deck of your account (in one of your folders; unlisted by default, so Commander Salt can score it) or go into one of your decks (adding its cards or replacing its list). Your own decks can be moved to another folder and made private, unlisted or public.
- **Decks tab:** three folding sections: Archidekt (yours), Archidekt (read only) and Local. The ⋮ menu has Bracket cards and Power level cards of all decks: small cards of your own decks, tap one for the full card and swipe between decks.
- **Every combo:** combos that don't count for the bracket are flagged too, in blue; red ones count. "COMBO 2" or "COMBO 3+" says how many cards. The deck says how many combos it has and how many count; each card lists its combos with a link to Commander Spellbook.
- **Casting the commander:** the deck stats show, turn by turn, how often the deck can cast its commander and has each colour, from 10,000 shuffles of the deck (lands, mana rocks, mana creatures and land ramp counted).
- **Update both phones:** the sync file changes with this version, so both phones need 1.46 before they sync again.

`MTG-Trader-1.46.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.46-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
