# 1.56: each attacking creature hits one opponent
- **Fixed: one big creature no longer knocks out the whole table.** The win-turn simulator added up the damage of all attackers (except commanders) and spread it over the opponents, so Ruby's Jumbo Cactuar (+9999/+0 when it attacks) knocked out all three opponents at once. Now each attacker hits one opponent: the biggest go first, each onto the opponent with the most life it can finish, or else the one with the least life. Nobody blocks, as before.
- Myriad copies still attack each opponent; with an extra combat a creature can attack someone else the second time; commander damage works as before.
- **Your decks (first win / 4 in 10 / 9 in 10, before → after):** Ruby's first win goes from turn 5 → **7** (in 10,000 games: wins by turn 5 1 → 0 in 100, by turn 6 3 → 0, by turn 8 14 → 9; still 4 in 10 by turn 10). Baba's first win 8 → 9. 9 in 10 games one turn later for Taranika band (12 → 13) and Tawnos' Army (12 → 13); The Plague of Grismold's 4 in 10 by turn 14 → 15. All other decks stay the same.
- Every deck's win turn is worked out again once after the update.
