# 1.35: mark wishlist cards as ordered
- **Ordered, on its way:** a wishlist card's window has a new checkbox, "Ordered, on its way". Tick it once you've bought the card; the app remembers the day.
- **See it in the list:** ordered cards show "📦 ordered" and the date under the card.
- **A fairer total:** the line above the wishlist counts ordered cards apart and only adds up what's still to buy, e.g. "12 card(s) wanted · 3 ordered · €30.00 still to buy".
- **Sharing the wishlist** leaves ordered cards out, so they aren't bought twice.
- **When it arrives:** add it to your collection as usual; "Remove the cards I got since adding them" takes it off the wishlist.
- **Both phones:** update both phones to 1.35 before they sync again. The mark is synced and kept in backups; a phone still on an older version is asked to update instead of syncing (the sync file's format went to 4), so it can't undo the marks.

`MTG-Trader-1.35.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.35-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
