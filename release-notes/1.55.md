# 1.55: a simulator that reads ramp, Treasures, the graveyard, copies, planeswalkers, Clues and counters
- **Winning the game reads much more of your decks.** Asked about Ruby, Quintorius, Poly Jace, Teval, Vohar Danse, Tawnos' Army, Haunted Ganax, Deep Clue Sea, Turtle Power, Smasher LCP, The Council of Four, Cloud and Zombies; the new rules come from the card text, so they work for any deck:
  - **mana:** ramp spells count every land (Ruby's 2-4-7), **Treasures are mana** (Ganax, Goldspan, Xorn), Dragon cost reducers, mana creatures with haste; a creature that taps for mana or for its ability (Vohar, Tawnos, Gilanra) **doesn't attack** that turn;
  - **pumps:** Craterhoof's and Moonshaker's +X/+X, End-Raze Forerunners and Jubilation, lords of a type (Quintorius's Spirits), Lathliss's pumps, Ruby's +2/+2, **Haunted One's +2/+0** for the Dragons;
  - **damage:** "each opponent discards a card and loses 2 life" (Arbiter of Woe, Malboro, Dusk Mangler), Archon of Cruelty, Gray Merchant, Dragon Tempest and Scourge of Valkas, Terror of the Peaks, Omnath's landfall tokens;
  - **the graveyard:** mill, looting (Vohar and Varina discard the creature to bring back), **reanimation** (Reanimate, Victimize, Living Death, Patriarch's Bidding, Zombie Apocalypse…), Rivaz and Chainer, flashback, and **Teval's and Quintorius's tokens** when cards leave the graveyard;
  - **copies, flicker and cheating:** Ghostly Flicker, Replication Technique, Tawnos's copies, **Jace's −3** and Mass Polymorph, Sneak Attack;
  - **planeswalkers** are cast and use a loyalty ability each turn (Jace, Quintorius, Elspeth, Liliana…);
  - **cascade and discover:** the spell found is cast for free and sets off its own triggers, so cascades chain (Ruby's Apex Devastator, Monstrous Vortex, Trumpeting Carnosaur, Aurora Phoenix; Omnath's Maelstrom Nexus); Smasher's **Improvisation Capstone** casts what it exiles and **paradigm** casts a free copy every turn after;
  - **Clues and cards drawn:** Clues are cracked, **Morska's counters** and the other "second card each turn" triggers, Tangletrove Kelp, Mechanized Production;
  - **+1/+1 counters:** **Leonardo**, Mutagens, Corpsejack Menace, High Score, Raphael;
  - **the opponents draw their cards**, so Howling Mine sets off **The Council of Four**, Faerie Mastermind and Scrawling Crawler;
  - Cloud's attack trigger (a card per equipped attacker, Treasures at power 7) and his free equip.
- **Casting the commander** counts both lands of Explosive Vegetation and the like, and Cultivate's land in hand.
- **Your decks (4 in 10 games by, before → after):** Turtle Power 12 → **8**, Deep Clue Sea 12 → **9**, Teval 12 → **9**, Quintorius 12 → **9**, Cloud 11 → **9**, Zombies 11 → **9**, Ruby 11 → **10** (first win turn 5 with Jumbo Cactuar; with cascade a win by turn 9 in 34 of 100 games instead of 25), Haunted Ganax 12 → **10**, Tawnos' Army 12 → **10**, The Council of Four 13 → **11**, Poly Jace 13 → **11**, Smasher LCP 13 → **12** (cascade and paradigm add 4–6 in 100 from turn 11), Vohar Danse 11 (9 in 10 by 12 now); Taranika band, Auntie Plague, Baba, Cult of Athreos, Plague of Grismold, Omnath and Sarge stay the same.
- The **How?** under Winning the game lists the new counts (graveyard, copies, planeswalkers, cascade).
- Planeswalkers' starting loyalty is now kept with the card details from Scryfall; your decks' planeswalkers get it at the next card-details update.
- Every deck’s win turn is worked out again once (a few seconds per deck; the games now run on several cores).

`MTG-Trader-1.55.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.55-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
