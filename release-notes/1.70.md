# 1.70: fairer win turns for token decks, spree and death triggers
- **Currency Converter** makes a Rogue or a Treasure only for a card actually exiled with it (one you discarded), instead of one every turn. Its loot and its token share the same tap.
- **Renewed Solidarity** copies only the tokens of the chosen type that entered this turn (the type your deck's tokens share most), and gives them +1/+0. Before, it made a token for every token you had, doubling the board each turn.
- **Spree** (Great Train Heist, Three Steps Ahead) pays for the modes it uses: those worth their extra mana.
- **"When this creature dies"** happens: Doomed Traveler's Spirit, Atsushi's Treasures, Vaultborn Tyrant's copy, Wilhelt's decayed Zombies (gone after they attack).
- **Wilderness Reclamation** untaps your lands at the end step for Clues and abilities you can use then.
- **Lands that make tokens** (Mirrex's Mites, Castle Ardenvale's Humans) use their ability with spare mana.
- Saved win turns are worked out again once.
