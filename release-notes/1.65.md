# 1.65: print the bracket and power level cards
- **Print cards as PDF:** the new 🖨 button at the top of "Bracket cards of all decks" and "Power level cards of all decks" makes an A4 PDF of the cards, to print and cut out at the size of a Magic card (63 × 88 mm). Nine cards fit on a page, touching, with cut marks in the margins. Print at 100% ("actual size").
- **Choose what goes in:** Bracket, Power level or Both (bracket cards first, then power level cards, each starting a new page), and which decks (all your own scored decks are ticked at first).
- **Ink saver:** light headers without the commander art, and lighter bars. The app remembers this and your choice of cards.
- **Preview, Print or Share:** Preview shows the real PDF (swipe between pages, pinch to zoom) with Print and Share PDF buttons, or print or share straight away. Share sends the PDF anywhere, for example to Signal or Molly's Note to Self.
- **The print cards** are shorter versions of the cards in the app. The bracket card has the brackets, the turn the deck wins by, the speed bars with the % of games won by each turn (first win in green, 4 in 10 games in gold), the bracket criteria, the game changers and the combos. The power level card has the power level and its meter, saltiness, the manabase, the main interaction and effects, and the win conditions. When a card has more combos than fit, it ends with "+1 more".
