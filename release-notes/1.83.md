# 1.83: retired decks
- **Retire a deck:** a deck you don't play any more can be retired (deck ⋮ → **Retire deck…**). It moves to a new **Retired** section at the bottom of the Decks tab and keeps its page, games and stats, but it leaves the seat pickers, **Update all decks** and the collection checks (the "in a deck" marks and the trade binder's "keep for my decks"). **Bring back** (on the deck, or ⋮) undoes it.
- **Show or hide retired decks:** the Retired section folds like the other sections, and Decks tab ⋮ → **Hide retired decks** hides it altogether.
- **Deleting a deck that played games** now says how many games it played and offers **Retire instead**. The games are kept either way.
- **On the website** too: Retire deck… / Bring back on the deck page, the Retired section with a show/hide button on the Decks list, and "Retire instead" when deleting. The website's Decks list sections now fold like the app's.
- **Please update every phone:** retired decks are a new synced field (sync format 13). A phone still on 1.82 pauses syncing until it is updated to 1.83; nothing is lost meanwhile.
