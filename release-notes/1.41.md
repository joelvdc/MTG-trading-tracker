# 1.41: changing a price's currency converts it
- **The currency button converts the price:** pick another currency in a price field and the amount changes with it, so it keeps its value: an alert at 300 kr. becomes 40,21 € (at the day's rate), not 300 €. Switch back and it's exactly 300,00 kr. again.
- **Everywhere there's a currency button:** the alert level on a wishlist card, a card's purchase price (its page and the card window), the agreed price in a trade, "Edit condition, language, price…", "Add to collection" from the wishlist and what a precon cost.
- **Typing in another currency:** pick the currency first, then type the amount. An empty field just changes its currency.
- **Nothing else changes:** saved prices and alerts stay as they are, and the two phones keep syncing with 1.39 and 1.40.

`MTG-Trader-1.41.apk` is for 64-bit ARM phones; use `MTG-Trader-1.41-universal.apk` if it won't install. Installs over any earlier version and keeps your data.
