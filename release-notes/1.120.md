# 1.120: Combo brackets and combo order from Commander Spellbook

- A combo now sets the deck's bracket by Commander Spellbook's own tag for it, the lowest bracket of the tag's range
  as a floor: Ruthless 4, Spicy and Powerful 3, Oddball and Core 2, Exhibition 1. This replaces the old rule (two-card
  combos, mana value, wins or not), and three-card combos now count too. "Why bracket" says which tag did it, e.g.
  "Tree of Perdition + Soul Immolation: Ruthless (bracket 4+)".
- The bracket card shows each combo's tag ("· Spicy"), and its criteria row is now "Combos".
- Win turn: combos are played in Commander Spellbook's order. A piece it lists as in hand has to be cast during the
  combo: Ondu Spiritdancer only copies itself endlessly when Secret Arcade is already unlocked, so Ondu first and the
  Room later no longer counts. Such a piece waits in hand while the rest of its combo is in sight, and a combo also
  needs the mana Commander Spellbook says it takes.
- Every deck's bracket and win turn are worked out again once, so some may move.
- Website: the same brackets, bracket card and win turn.
