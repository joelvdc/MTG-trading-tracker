# 1.116: One bracket card everywhere, power level gone

- One bracket card, the size of a Magic card (63 × 88 mm), the same on the phone's screen, on the website and printed:
  Bracket · Salt · Wins by at the top, why that bracket, the speed bars, the bracket criteria, the deck's lands
  (MDFCs as "+2"), ramp, draw, removal, wipes and tutors (amber when outside the guideline), game changers, combos and,
  when there's room, the saltiest cards.
- Bracket cards print nine to an A4 page with cut marks, from the app and from the website (Decks → Bracket cards),
  with an ink saver option.
- Deck scores are now Bracket (with its name), Salt (with its level) and Wins by (with the first winning turn).
- Every deck's bracket is estimated by the app from its list: edhpowerlevel.com and the power level are gone,
  and so are the Power card and the Bracket from setting. Decks rate themselves when needed, no login to the
  website required.
- Lands on the card count from Scryfall's type lines, the same as the Land heading.
- Old scores stay stored, so phones on older versions keep syncing.
