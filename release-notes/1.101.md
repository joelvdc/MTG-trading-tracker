# 1.101: a faster win turn, same answers
- **Faster win turn**: working out a deck's simulated win turn takes less time, in the app and on the website. Most of the time went into choosing which land to play each turn; that choice is now worked out without redoing the same work for every land in hand, and decks without Renewed Solidarity no longer check for it in every game. On the website a deck takes about half the time it did (10,000 games of the test deck: 23 s → 10 s on one PC core); on the phone the gain is smaller (about a fifth).
- **Exactly the same results**: every game plays out as before, so the win turns you already have stay valid and nothing is recalculated. Checked on all 21 decks in the Ready to Play folder at 10,000 games each: every count matched.
- **On the website (already live)**: the playtester's big card picture now appears when the mouse rests on a card, in the battlefield's top-right corner; select several cards by dragging a box on the empty battlefield (Ctrl-click adds one) and drag, tap, untap or move them together, as one undo step (right-click for the group menu, T to tap or untap); a dotted grid that dropped cards snap to (on/off in the battlefield's right-click menu); a tapped card stays turned while you drag it.
- No sync change: phones on 1.97 and later keep syncing.
